What We Do
Our Services
Complete mechanical and crane engineering solutions — from on-site field repair to in-house precision machining.
01
Crane Repair & Overhaul
Full diagnosis and repair of EOT cranes, mobile cranes, tower cranes, and jib cranes. All mechanisms — hoisting, travel, slewing — restored to operational standard.
02
Hydraulic System Services
Cylinder reconditioning, pump overhaul, valve replacement, pressure testing, and seal replacement. Complete hydraulic crane maintenance by a trained specialist.
03
Precision Parts Fabrication
Custom-machined replacement parts manufactured when OEM parts are unavailable. Lathe, milling, drilling, and grinding to exact tolerances using industry-grade machinery.
04
Mechanical Fitting & Assembly
Expert fitting and re-assembly of gearboxes, drum assemblies, brake mechanisms, rope systems, and structural crane joints with precision fitting skills.
05
Breakdown Emergency Response
Rapid on-site response for unexpected crane failures. Fast diagnosis and parts replacement to minimize costly downtime at your facility or construction site.
06
Annual Maintenance Contracts
Structured AMC plans covering scheduled inspections, lubrication, load testing, and full documentation. Ideal for factories, logistics hubs, and construction firms.

About Us
Built on Precision.
Proven in the Field.

Siddiqui Engineering Works is a Mumbai-based mechanical engineering firm specialising in the complete repair, fabrication, and maintenance of all types of mechanical and hydraulic cranes — from tower and overhead cranes to mobile, EOT and industrial hoisting systems.

The company was recently established to bring our work officially into the market — but our real experience goes back to 2003. For over 22 years, our founder has been delivering high-quality crane engineering services across Maharashtra.

Our strongest advantage is our complete in-house workshop capability — from manufacturing brand-new parts on lathe, milling and grinding machines to full hydraulic system diagnostics and overhaul. No outsourcing, no middlemen.

// Founder & Lead Engineer
Nadeem Ahmad Siddiqui
Crane Engineer · Mechanical & Hydraulic Systems Specialist

Nadeem has been working in crane and mechanical engineering since 2003 — over 22 years of hands-on field and workshop experience. He has mastered every major crane type and machining discipline, working with leading crane companies and infrastructure contractors across India.

His ability to design and manufacture new parts from raw materials — combined with deep expertise in hydraulic systems — makes him one of the most complete crane engineers in the region.
